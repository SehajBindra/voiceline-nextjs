# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are two equal audiences:

- People booking Voiceline Studio in Delhi for voiceover, music, podcast, video, dubbing, or other audio production.
- People seeking Hudson Voice Technique training — corporate professionals, educators, and aspiring voice artists.

## Product Purpose

Voiceline Studio is a Delhi recording studio and voice-training house. It exists so clients can record and finish audio to a professional standard, and so trainees can refine their speaking voice. Success for this surface is a visitor booking a studio session.

## Positioning

A physical recording studio in Shahpur Jat, Delhi, with in-house engineering plus Hudson Voice Technique UK training. Neighboring studios can copy rooms and gear; they cannot truthfully claim this studio’s Hudson partnership and the owners’ voice-artist / training practice.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically arrive from search for a Delhi recording studio, voiceover work, or voice training. They evaluate the space, services, and training, then book or contact the studio. Contact channels already in use: email (`info@voiceline.in`), phone (`+91 966-700-2480`, `+91 935-018-8055`), WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook. Proof lives on the existing show reel and testimonials pages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed services: vocal and voiceover recording, music production / mixing / mastering, podcast recording, dubbing, sound editing and design, foley, background music, and voiceover in Indian languages with a focus on neutral Hindi and English.

Confirmed spaces: recording room, music production / editing room, podcast room.

Confirmed gear (do not invent additional kit): AKG C2, Rode NT series, Focusrite Scarlett 6i6, Yamaha MG06, Sennheiser HD 560S, Yamaha HS5, FabFilter, Waves 10, Presonus plugins, Studio One 5, acoustic treatment.

Primary call to action for the current marketing hero: book a studio session.

This repository is a Next.js + shadcn/ui rebuild of the public marketing site. Only the hero is in scope for the current build.

## Product Principles

1. Convert first on booking a session; training is an equal audience, not a competing story.
2. Stay geographically and physically honest — this is a Delhi studio, not a software product.
3. Say less than the current site; visitors should scan purpose, place, and action in seconds.
4. Use only real people, real services, and real proof already on hand.
5. Hudson Voice Technique is the training claim; do not dilute or genericize it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific accessibility standard was established. Default to perceivable, operable marketing UI: real text, visible focus, and respect for reduced motion.
