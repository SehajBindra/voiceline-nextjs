## Intent

A storytelling experience video for Voiceline Studio that sells the *feeling* of finding a room that listens — not microphones, pricing, or service menus. Hand-drawn doodle aesthetic in the brand's blue and yellow palette from `app/globals.css`. Out-of-distribution, sketchbook warmth: wobbly ink lines, yellow highlight washes, cream paper texture. The arc follows a voice from silence → weight → threshold → listen → release → Voiceline.

## Assets

- app/globals.css — brand color tokens (primary blue, chart yellow)
- PRODUCT.md — studio truth (Shahpur Jat, Delhi; Voiceline identity)

## Customizations

- Hand-drawn SVG doodles (microphone, door, speech bubble, tangled lines, waveform) with stroke-draw animation
- Experience-first storytelling arc; no invented stats, clients, or gear claims
